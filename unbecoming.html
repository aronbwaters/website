The mirror

This isn't about fixing yourself. You were never broken.

You've read the books. Done the journaling. Tried the meditation apps. Maybe even started therapy. And something still doesn't land.

Not because you're not doing enough. Because you're doing the wrong kind of work.

You keep adding. New habits. New routines. New frameworks. New goals on top of old wounds. And then you wonder why nothing sticks.

Here's what nobody told you.

You can't build on top of what was never you.

The roles you're playing. The beliefs you inherited. The version of success someone else designed for you. The voice that says "I should be grateful" while something inside you is screaming.

That's not you. That's the layers.

And no amount of adding will fix what needs removing.

The reframe

The real work isn't becoming more. It's becoming less.

Knowledge is adding things. Wisdom is removing things.

Everything you learned to be. The responsible one. The strong one. The one who never complains. The one who holds it together. The one who says yes when they mean no.

Those aren't personality traits. Those are survival strategies. You built them in childhood because they worked. They got you love. They got you safety. They got you ahead.

And now they're running your life. On autopilot. Without your permission.

The Unbecoming is the work of stripping them away. Layer by layer. Until what's real has room to breathe.

Not adding a new version of you on top of the old one. Removing the old one entirely.

That's where the freedom lives.

What this looks like in real life

She tried to go back to her old life. It wouldn't take her.

She came to the work living a life that wasn't hers.

From the outside, everything looked fine. From the inside, she was running someone else's program. Following rules she didn't choose. Pleasing people she didn't agree with. Performing a version of herself that worked for everyone except her.

My content woke something up. She started to dive deeper. And what she found was not what she expected.

Emotions she had buried since childhood came forward. Parts of herself she didn't know existed. Memories she had pushed so far down she forgot they were there. She went through what you could call a dark night of the soul. Layer after layer after layer.

It was not comfortable. It was not fast.

But slowly, something started to shift.

Joy came back. Not performed joy. Real joy. Creativity returned. She started making decisions she had never made before. Small ones at first. Then bigger ones. She stopped people-pleasing. And then she started calling out the things that didn't sit right with her. Out loud. For the first time.

The moment she knew it was real was when she tried to go back to her old life. Again and again. But it wouldn't take her. She couldn't pretend anymore. The work had changed her. Not from the outside. From the inside.

Her whole environment was screaming for her to come back to who she was before. They didn't recognize her. But to her, she was finally coming back. To herself.

Slowly. Alive. More present. Choosing herself.

She had the same job for years. She thought she was going to do it forever. Now she's allowing herself to dream about something else. It's not tangible yet. But it's growing. There's no forcing. Just allowing.

There wasn't one moment it all changed. It was a gradual process. And she's still learning. Learning to choose herself. Learning to love herself. Learning to live a life that's actually hers.

That's what the Unbecoming does.

The course

Six steps back to who you actually are.

Self-paced. In your own time. As deep as you're ready to go.

01

The Layers

See what's been covering your core. The roles. The rules. The version of you someone else designed. Before you can remove anything, you need to see it clearly.

02

Subtraction

Strip away what was never yours. You can't build on top of what isn't you. So first, we take away. This is where the real work begins.

03

The Inner Child

Go back to the child you were before the world got to you. Meet them. Listen. This is where the healing lives. Not in the head. In the feeling.

04

Inherited Beliefs

Name the beliefs that were given, not chosen. The ones you carry without knowing why. The ones that feel like truth but were someone else's fear. Then question them.

05

The Release

Practices to let go. Not mentally. In the body. Because what lives in the body cannot be thought away. It has to be felt through.

06

The Clearing

What's left when everything that wasn't you is gone. This is the ground to build on. This is where the remembering starts.

FAQ

Before you decide.

How long does the course take?+

It's self-paced. Most people move through it in 4 to 6 weeks. But there's no deadline. Go at the speed your body needs, not the speed your head demands.

Do I need any experience with inner work?+

No. This course meets you where you are. Whether you've done years of healing or you're just starting to look inward, the tools work the same way.

Is this a replacement for coaching?+

It's a different thing. The course gives you the tools and the process to start the Unbecoming on your own. Coaching gives you a guide walking beside you in real time. Many people start with the course and move into coaching when they're ready to go deeper.

What if it brings up a lot of emotion?+

Good. That means it's working. The course is designed to help you move through what comes up, not push it back down. If you need more support, coaching is the next step.

Is this only for women?+

No. This work is for anyone who feels like something is off and is ready to look inward. Men and women, 25 to 55, who know there's more underneath the performance.

Can I get a refund?+

Due to the digital nature of the course, refunds are not available. But if you do the work, it will meet you. That's a promise built on ten years of walking this path myself.
