The mirror

You've been looking in the wrong place.

You've taken the personality tests. Read the career books. Maybe even hired a coach. And you still can't name it. The thing. Your thing. The gift that's yours and nobody else's.

Not because it isn't there. Because you've been looking for it in all the wrong places.

You've been looking in your skills. In your CV. In what people tell you you're good at. In what the market rewards.

But your genius doesn't live there.

Your genius lives in the thing that costs you nothing. The thing that feels like play to you but looks like magic to everyone else. The thing you do without trying. The thing people keep coming to you for and you keep dismissing because it feels too easy.

You've been ignoring it your whole life. Because nobody told you that easy is the signal.

The reframe

Your genius is not what you do best. It's what costs you nothing.

We were taught that value comes from effort. That if it's hard, it matters. That if it comes naturally, it doesn't count.

That's the lie that keeps people stuck.

Your genius is the opposite of effort. It's the thing that flows. The thing you'd do for free. The thing you lose track of time doing. The thing that lights you up from the inside and makes the people around you wonder how you do it so easily.

It's not a luxury. It's not a hobby. It's the most valuable thing you have.

And the moment you build your life around it instead of around what you should be doing, everything changes. You stop competing. You stop forcing. You stop burning out.

Because you can't burn out doing the thing you were born to do.

What this looks like in real life

She thought her gift was too simple to matter.

A woman came to this work thinking she needed to find something impressive. A big purpose. A grand calling. Something that would justify changing her life.

What she found was cooking.

Not restaurant cooking. Not career cooking. Intuitive cooking. Making dishes for people she loved. Following her hands instead of a recipe. Tasting, adjusting, creating something beautiful from nothing.

She almost dismissed it. Too simple. Too ordinary. Not enough.

But when she stopped dismissing and started following, something happened. The joy didn't just stay in the kitchen. It spread. She started making other decisions from the same place. Intuition instead of obligation. What felt right instead of what looked right.

She moved into a new home. For the first time in her life, she furnished it entirely on her own terms. Her whole life, her mother had decided. So choosing felt terrifying. Can I do this? Am I doing it right?

The result was stunning. And entirely hers.

Along with the joy came ownership. Along with the ownership came power. Not the forced kind. The kind that comes from finally knowing: this is mine.

That's what discovering your genius does. Not a career change. A permission change. The permission to follow what's actually alive in you. And build from there.

The course

Five weeks to remember what was always yours.

Guided. Structured. One step per week. Deep enough to shift something real.

"Creativity is intelligence having fun." — Albert Einstein

01

Subtraction

Let go of everything that no longer serves you. Strip away what you're not. Before you can discover your genius, you need to clear the noise that's covering it. This is where we make room.

02

Remember

Go back to your inner child. What gave you pure joy before the world got to you? What did you do for hours without noticing time? That's not nostalgia. That's data.

03

Passion and Joy

Map what makes you lose track of time. What gives you energy. What you never get enough of. Joy is not frivolous. Joy is directional. It's the most reliable compass you have.

04

IKIGAI

Find your reason for being. The intersection of what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for. This is where the four circles lock together. This is the genius zone.

05

Dreams and Desires

Create your North Star vision. Not a goal. A compass. If everything was possible, who would you be? What would you build? This is where feeling becomes direction.

FAQ

Before you decide.

How is this different from a personality test or career quiz?+

Those tell you what type you are. This course helps you feel what's actually alive in you and build from it. The difference is between information and transformation.

Do I need to have done the UNBECOMING course first?+

No. They work independently. UNBECOMING clears what's in the way. GENIUS discovers what's underneath. Some people do both. Some start here. Both work.

What if I already know my gift but can't seem to do anything with it?+

Then you're probably closer than you think. Week 5 is specifically about turning the knowing into a direction. Your North Star. Not a plan. A compass.

Is this course guided or self-paced?+

Self-paced. One week per module is the recommended rhythm, but you can go at whatever speed your life allows. The exercises are what matter, not the speed.

What if I get stuck?+

The course is designed to meet you where you are. If you feel stuck, that's usually the sign you're at the edge of something real. Sit with it. If you want personal guidance through it, 1:1 coaching is the next step.

Is this only for people who want to change careers?+

No. Most people who do this work don't quit their job. They change their relationship to it. They find their gift and start living from it. Sometimes that means a career shift. Sometimes it means bringing your genius into the life you already have.
